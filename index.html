Featured Case Study

Growing Organic Traffic for Dhaka Mart Fashion

Dhaka Mart is a clothing brand's e-commerce platform in Bangladesh. As IT Executive handling website operations and SEO, I led the end-to-end organic search strategy from product page optimization to category-level keyword targeting.

  • 1Conducted keyword research targeting high-intent queries like "bridal saree price in bangladesh", "wedding saree price in bangladesh" achieving top-10 rankings for competitive fashion keywords.
  • 2Optimized category pages (bridal saree, party saree, gadwal saree) with proper meta titles, descriptions, and structured content — improving category page CTR to 9.58%.
  • 3Executed on-page SEO across 1,000+ product listings including product descriptions, image alt texts, and internal linking structure.
  • 4Published SEO-targeted blog content (e.g., Eid Collection 2026 guide) that ranked and drove 95+ additional organic clicks.
  • 5Result: Grew organic clicks from 1,631/month (Dec 2025) to 2,405/month (May 2026). CTR rose from 4.93% to 8.34%. Average position improved from 10.3 to 7.6. Site now visible in 50+ countries.
